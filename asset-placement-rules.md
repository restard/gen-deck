# Asset Placement Rules

Last updated: June 6, 2026

## Purpose

Naturally place provided photos, images, worksheets, QR codes, and similar assets onto the provided background. Do not alter the background or assets on your own.

Keep these rules as short and simple as possible. Instead of adding detailed conditions, prioritize placing the provided materials without changing them.

## Highest-Priority Rule

Do not change the attached assets themselves. Use the provided background, people, photos, text, QR codes, tables, and worksheets as they are.

The only changes allowed are size, position, and minimal cropping needed for natural placement.

Exceptions:

- You may adjust the size and position of assets to fit frames or whitespace
- You may apply minimal cropping to make the placement look natural
- For multiple photos, you may align their aspect ratios, sizes, and visual appearance
- For comparisons such as Before/After, you may arrange the assets so the relationship is clear

However, do not redraw the background or assets, and do not add a different background, decoration, person, photo, or text.

## Workflow

When the user says something like "作って", "はめて", "貼り付けて", "このまま進めて", or similar wording, place the attached assets without repeated additional confirmation.

Ask a short clarifying question only when it is unclear which file is the background, which assets should be placed, where each asset should go, or how the assets correspond to each other.
